US English Video Recording Project

Submit 2–3 spontaneous “free talk” video samples (2–3 minutes each) to qualify for our upcoming US English video collection project. Paid project recordings will be 10 minutes each.

Project Overview

Before qualifying for the project, we require 2–3 sample videos of spontaneous “free talk” — speaking naturally on any topic without a script.

Each video must begin with the required opening line: “This is my free speech video for the US video recording project.”

Critical Fail Conditions

Submissions will be automatically rejected if they contain: no speech; incorrect framing/orientation (face not visible); duration under 2 minutes; distracting or non-compliant background; non-compliant clothing; non-US accent; scripted or non-fluent speech; stuttering.

Video Requirements

  • 1

    Content, Pose & Expression

    2–3 minutes per sample video (full paid project recordings will be 10 minutes each); spontaneous, unscripted speech directly to camera; natural pauses of 5–7 seconds are fine.

  • 2

    Framing & Camera Setup

    Landscape orientation; subject centered in portrait-style (head-and-shoulders) framing; camera at eye level, 1–1.5 metres away, stable framing.

  • 3

    Expression & Movement

    Neutral expression or slight smile only; minimal body/head movement (natural blinking only).

  • 4

    Background, Lighting & Styling

    Studio setup preferred, minimal background noise; even lighting with no harsh shadows; business-casual or neutral attire with no distracting patterns or logos.

  • 5

    Technical Specifications

    MP4 (H.264), 1080p, minimum 25 FPS (60 FPS preferred); synchronized audio, minimum 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono; SNR 30–40 dB.
